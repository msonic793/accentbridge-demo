AccentBridge Prototype-Anchored Exemplar Conditioning for Accent Control in Zero-Shot TTS

Anonymous submission · Audio samples

AccentBridge takes the voice and the accent as two separate examples: a reference prompt fixes who is speaking, while short exemplar clips of any speaker fix the accent. A single guidance weight w, chosen after training, sets how strongly the accent is applied. The system is one model and one forward pass, with no accent label and no conversion stage after synthesis.

1Accent transfer

Each row keeps the speaker from the voice prompt and takes the accent from the accent reference, a recording by a different person. All three systems receive identical inputs and generate the same unseen sentence. Headphones recommended.

What to listen for. F5-TTS has no accent control, so it renders the prompt speaker's own accent. The cascade converts the waveform with a separate voice-conversion model, which moves the timbre away from the prompt. AccentBridge changes the accent while the voice stays the prompt's.

2Continuous accent strength

The same model and the same exemplar clips at increasing guidance weight. Nothing is retrained between columns; w is a single scalar applied at synthesis time.

Audio is 24 kHz mono WAV. Accent references are drawn from Common Voice; voice prompts come from a licensed voice bank. Samples are generated at w as labelled.
